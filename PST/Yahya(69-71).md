## Background of Yahya Khan Era (1969–1971)

* Yahya Khan imposed Martial Law in March 1969.
* He inherited severe political and economic instability.
* He promised to restore democracy.
* He faced intense regional and ethnic tensions.

## Legal Framework Order (LFO) 1970

* It was issued to transition Pakistan to civilian rule.
* It provided the legal basis for general elections.
* It recognized the right of adult franchise.
* It maintained strong presidential powers.

## General Elections 1970

* These were the first elections based on adult franchise.
* The Awami League won a majority in the National Assembly.
* The Pakistan People’s Party (PPP) emerged as the major party in West Pakistan.
* The failure to transfer power led to a political deadlock.

## Political Crisis and Breakdown

* A major dispute arose over the Awami League's Six Points formula.
* The postponement of the National Assembly session triggered the crisis.
* Mass protests and civil disobedience erupted in East Pakistan.
* Mistrust grew rapidly between East and West Pakistan.

## Military Operation in East Pakistan

* The army launched Operation Searchlight in March 1971.
* Military force was used to suppress political opposition.
* Large-scale human rights violations occurred.
* The operation caused the mass displacement of civilians.

## Role of India and Indo-Pak War 1971

* India provided support to the Mukti Bahini rebels.
* The mass displacement created a massive refugee crisis in India.
* A full-scale war broke out in December 1971.
* Pakistan faced international diplomatic isolation.

## Fall of Dhaka 1971

* Pakistani forces surrendered on December 16, 1971.
* This resulted in the creation of Bangladesh.
* Pakistan suffered the permanent loss of East Pakistan.
* It was a severe blow to national morale.

## Consequences of Dismemberment

* The defeat marked the end of the Yahya Khan regime.
* Power was transferred to Zulfikar Ali Bhutto.
* It created an urgent need for constitutional and political reconstruction.
* It left a permanent impact on Pakistan’s national security thinking.
