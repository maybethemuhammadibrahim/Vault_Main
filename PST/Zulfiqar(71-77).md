## Rise of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto and the PPP

* Zulfikar Ali Bhutto formed the Pakistan People’s Party (PPP) with the slogan "roti, kapra, makaan" (food, clothing, shelter).
* The party's core motives were: Islam is our Faith, Democracy is our Policy, and Socialism is our Economy.
* Bhutto served as Foreign Minister under Ayub Khan and influenced the launch of Operation Gibraltar (1965) in Kashmir.
* After the 1965 war and the Moscow peace treaty, Bhutto turned against Ayub Khan, resigned, and built his own political base.
* Following the 1971 electoral deadlock and the creation of Bangladesh, Bhutto became the first Civilian Chief Martial Law Administrator.

## The 1973 Constitution

* Passed on August 14, 1973, establishing Pakistan as an "Islamic Republic" with a federal system.
* Introduced a strong parliamentary form of government, giving maximum power to the Prime Minister and weakening the President.
* Ensured fundamental rights, an independent judiciary, and a bicameral legislature (Senate and National Assembly).
* Declared Urdu as the national language and English as the official language.

## Key Constitutional Amendments Under Bhutto

* **1st Amendment:** Acknowledged Bangladesh as a separate independent nation.
* **2nd Amendment:** Declared Ahmadis as non-Muslims following widespread protests.
* **3rd, 4th, & 5th Amendments:** Expanded executive power, restricted the judiciary from granting bail to political opponents, and limited judicial interference.
* **6th Amendment:** Extended the retirement age for the Chief Justices of the Supreme and High Courts.
* **7th Amendment:** Allowed the Prime Minister to hold a national referendum.

## Policy Reforms

* **Education & Health:** Nationalized educational institutions, made basic education free, established new medical colleges, and mandated written prescriptions from doctors.
* **Industrial Nationalization:** Placed 34 major industries (iron, steel, cement, etc.) under government control via the Board of Industrial Management (BIM).
* **Economic Impact:** Nationalization later expanded to ghee, rice, and cotton industries, which ultimately reduced investment and led to inefficiencies under government management.
* **Land Reforms:** Lowered land ownership ceilings to 150 irrigated acres and 300 unirrigated acres.
* **Agricultural Relief:** Exempted taxes for landholdings under 12 acres and redistributed seized feudal lands to landless peasants.

## Foreign Policy Achievements

* **Shimla Agreement (1972):** Signed with Indian PM Indira Gandhi to resolve conflicts bilaterally, respect the Line of Control, and peacefully withdraw troops.
* **Recognition of Bangladesh:** Officially recognized Bangladesh to secure the release of 93,000 Pakistani Prisoners of War (Delhi Agreement).
* **Nuclear Program:** Initiated Pakistan's secret nuclear development program to counter India's nuclear threat, despite strict warnings from the United States.

## Downfall and Execution

* Bhutto faced severe opposition from regional leaders, religious groups, and the Pakistan National Alliance (PNA).
* He formed the Federal Security Force (FSF) to suppress opposition and manage domestic crises.
* The 1977 elections were heavily disputed, with the PNA accusing Bhutto of massive vote-rigging, leading to violent nationwide protests.
* General Zia-ul-Haq, appointed Army Chief by Bhutto, imposed Martial Law in 1977 and suspended the constitution.
* Bhutto was arrested, tried, and found guilty of the murder of a political opponent's father.
* He was denied the right to appeal or receive foreign intervention and was executed by hanging in 1979.
