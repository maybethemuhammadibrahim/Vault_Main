### 1. The Three "Spaces" of a Matrix

Every matrix contains three hidden geometric shapes (subspaces). These shapes tell us everything about what the matrix can and cannot do.

* **Row Space:** The "Active Inputs." Which parts of your input vector actually affect the output?
* **Column Space:** The "Reachable Destinations." What outputs can the matrix produce?
* **Null Space:** The "Ignored Inputs." Which inputs get crushed to zero?
